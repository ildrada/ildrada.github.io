Privacy policy

JSCOPILOT local · last updated 4 November 2026

Who uses it

Only the owner of this site. The app has no other users and is not offered to the public.

What data it accesses

  • Gmail: job-alert e-mails and the owner's own messages to the assistant; it sends e-mails only to the owner's own address and labels them.
  • Google Drive and Google Sheets: the files and spreadsheets the owner uses for the job search.

How the data is used

Only to search for jobs, track applications and prepare documents for the owner. When the owner chooses a remote AI model for a step, the text needed for that step is sent to that model's provider for processing. Nothing is sold, shared for advertising or used to train models by this app.

Where the data is stored

In the owner's own Google account and on the owner's own computer.

Google API Services

The use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Removing access

Access can be withdrawn at any time in the Google Account, under Security, "Third-party apps & services".
